`work/p90-native-port/live-test/2026-08-10-native-debian/`. The probes issue
only information ioctls and a read-only framebuffer mapping; they do not claim
display ownership or consume touch events.

`outputs/p90-native-linux-port/scripts/build-xorg-fbdev-package-bundle.sh`.

build VM, and writes per-package SHA-256 values. The retained bundle is
`work/p90-native-port/package-bundles/xorg-fbdev-bookworm.tar.gz`; installation
evidence is `work/p90-native-port/live-test/2026-08-10-native-debian/xorg-fbdev-install.txt`.
